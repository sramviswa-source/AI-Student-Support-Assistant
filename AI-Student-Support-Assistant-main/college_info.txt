1. COLLEGE OVERVIEW
============================================================

College Name:
Jayaraj Annapackiam CSI College Of Engineering

Location:
Tuticorin, Tamil Nadu, India

The college is a higher education institution providing
undergraduate engineering and technology programs.

The campus provides academic facilities, computer laboratories,
engineering laboratories, library services, transportation,
placement assistance, sports facilities, student clubs, and
other support services.

The college promotes technical education, practical learning,
professional development, innovation, and career development.

13. ATTENDANCE POLICY
============================================================

Students are required to maintain a minimum attendance of 75%.

Students whose attendance falls below the prescribed minimum
may not be permitted to appear for university examinations,
subject to applicable university regulations.

Attendance between 65% and 74% may be considered for
condonation according to applicable university regulations.

Students should regularly monitor their attendance.

Attendance is recorded for both theory and practical classes.

Students are responsible for maintaining sufficient attendance
throughout the semester.


============================================================
14. LEAVE POLICY
============================================================

Students who require leave should submit a leave request to
their class advisor or concerned department.

General leave procedure:

1. Inform the concerned faculty member.
2. Submit the required leave application.
3. Provide supporting documents if required.
4. Obtain approval from the appropriate authority.

Medical leave may require a valid medical certificate or
supporting medical documents.

Leave approval is subject to college and university rules.

Students should understand that approved leave does not
necessarily remove attendance requirements.
